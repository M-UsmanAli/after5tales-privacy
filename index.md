# Privacy Policy for After 5 Tales

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026

After 5 Tales ("the app") is a collection of bedtime stories, motivation and guides, published by M Usman Ali (codeafter_5).

## 1. Information we collect
The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts or login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

## 2. Content
All stories and text in the app are stored inside the app itself. The app does not need to download content from the internet to work.

## 3. Advertising and analytics
The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

## 4. Children
Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either.

## 5. Third parties
The app does not share any data with third parties. If you downloaded the app from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy.

## 6. Changes to this policy
If we add features in the future that collect or use data (for example accounts, advertising or analytics), we will update this policy before releasing them and change the effective date above.
